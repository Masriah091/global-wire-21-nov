# THE GLOBAL WIRE - The World in One Day, Saturday 21 November 2026

A wire-service monochrome front page: one picture carrying the whole of
Saturday 21 November 2026 - 40 stories from 7 regions, every one dated
that Saturday. Missiles landing in Poland, COP31 slipping into overtime,
Wall Street's fall into correction and Denmark's exposed plot lead; 13
pictured stories sit over a 27-brief "Also on" wire, a ruled table
gives the day in numbers, and a pin map drawn from real country
outlines shows where it all happened. Every story carries its source
beside it (AP, BBC, Reuters, ICC and more), contested claims are
attributed, and a red DATE CHECK box names the Friday 20 November
stories deliberately left out. All artwork is original drawings - flat
geometric scenes composed in code, no photos. The page is produced by
draw.py, a deterministic Pillow program (Python stdlib + PIL only, no
network, no AI images) that renders entry.png at 1024x1024; main.py
copies it to the output directory.
